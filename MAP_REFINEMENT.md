# Map generator 2.1 — refined geography
Research branch only. Integrates generator in the branch app and replaces the flat rectangle with the original unmarked lower parchment panel (full-width copy). Main remains unchanged.
## Refinement
Increased high-frequency coastline variation and directional bay/peninsula depth. Stronger spatial growth noise, directional traversal cost, and a connected local border-warp step add irregularity without introducing separate islands or detached provinces. Warp intensity decreases on retries; rejected maps never reach gameplay.
## Verified development sample
Independent validator, seeds 1–100: 100 passed, 0 failed. 1,800 provinces. Mean height/width 1.246; area ratio 2.912; coast complexity 1.916; border complexity 9.464; mean degree 4.066; mean generation attempts 11.7 (maximum 39). Previous v2 prototype's 1,000-seed means: 1.289, 3.044, 1.662, 8.208, 4.125 and 2.481 attempts respectively. Thus coastline complexity +15.3% and border complexity +15.3%, relative to v2; relative to the initial generator's 50-seed comparison, +26.1% coastline and +16.3% internal border complexity. Samples differ in size; descriptive comparisons, not independent inference. Area variation decreased slightly versus v2, remains above initial generator's 2.365 mean. Browser rendering and phone performance remain to be tested. This refinement was validated on 100 seeds, not 1,000; the first prototype's 1,000 tests and 600 gameplay comparisons do not automatically validate v2.1.
## Integration
Only random-map mode uses the new module. England geometry is untouched. Smaller random castle exclusion rectangles reflect actual artwork, retaining separate castle/horse anchors. The research branch app copies the existing blank parchment artwork as its upper map panel instead of the solid rectangle. The change has been syntax-checked but not visually verified on a phone. Do not deploy to main until review and further validation.
