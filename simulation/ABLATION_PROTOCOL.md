# Recruitment ablation protocol (development only)
- M0: frozen baseline.
- M1: when no viable conquest plan exists and the lord is safely at home, plan recruitment toward a reachable hostile frontier. Use projected combat odds and no fixed total soldier ceiling. Buy soldiers, plus a first catapult if required.
- M2: identical blocked-plan intervention and target selection, but cap additional soldier-equivalent strength at 75 (Norman) or 34 (Saxon); optimize soldier, knight and catapult mix, allowing up to five catapults.
- M3: adaptive strength plus composition optimization, no fixed total ceiling.
- All challengers share a 25-gold monthly preparation budget, three-gold reserve, target odds of attack floor +8 percentage points, same target ranking and safe-frontier search. They do not intervene if a viable current plan exists or home danger >=45%. Existing priority first-catapult rule runs first; normal defense, opening expansion, alliances, combat and route execution are unchanged. This is a 2×2-style architectural screen, not an exact single-factor test because M0 lacks the shared blocked-target planning hook. M1 vs M3 isolates composition conditional on adaptive recruitment; M2 vs M3 isolates lifting the fixed ceiling conditional on composition.
- First screen: 50 development seeds × 3 difficulties × 2 maps × 8 policies = 2,400 per model. 9,600 total. Same seeds and policies, original shared combat RNG can diverge. No validation/holdout.
- Primary metrics: defensive-policy unresolved fraction at 60 months; overall stall proxy; guard fraction. Check expert-policy human win rate per difficulty, opening claims and errors. No automatic deployment; stronger AI may require difficulty tuning. Desired win ranges remain provisional; do not optimize against passive or purely defensive win rate.
- Diagnostic metrics: soldiers/knights/catapults bought, preparation turns, siege timing, Norman infighting.
- Composition search enumerates all legal purchases within the per-turn budget; picks cheapest package meeting desired projected odds or largest odds improvement if unattainable this month. It is a local incremental optimizer, not a full horizon cost optimizer. Knights may be dominated by soldiers under present combat economics. Target selection doesn't forecast future enemy recruitment; path planning and diplomacy will be addressed separately.
